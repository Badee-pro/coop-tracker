# Co-op Tracker: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 22, 2026_

Co-op Tracker (https://badee-pro.github.io/coop-tracker/) and its browser extension, Co-op Tracker Sync, help you track your own University of Waterloo co-op applications. Co-op Tracker is a personal project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the University of Waterloo.

## Where your data lives

Co-op Tracker has **no server, no database and no accounts**. Everything (your synced applications, your notes and the applications you add yourself) is saved only in your own browser's local storage on your device. The author of Co-op Tracker never receives it.

Clearing your browser data, or using another browser or device, starts you with an empty tracker; syncing again brings your WaterlooWorks applications back.

## What the extension reads

When you click **Sync from WaterlooWorks**, the extension reads your applications list on `waterlooworks.uwaterloo.ca` in the tab Co-op Tracker opened. For each application this includes the job title, job ID, term, organization, application status, job status, division, location, city, number of openings, application deadline and submission date.

It only reads WaterlooWorks in a tab that Co-op Tracker opened for a sync. It does not read other tabs or other websites, and it does nothing on WaterlooWorks pages you open yourself.

## What happens to the data

The applications are passed directly from the WaterlooWorks tab to the Co-op Tracker page in the same browser, which saves them in that browser. Co-op Tracker and the extension:

- do **not** send your data to the author or any third party
- do **not** read or store your WaterlooWorks password or cookies
- do **not** use analytics, advertising or tracking
- do **not** sell or share data

The website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which receives standard request information (such as your IP address) when the page loads, as with any website using it.

## Permissions

- **tabs**: to recognize the WaterlooWorks tab that Co-op Tracker opened, and close it when the sync is done.
- **storage**: to remember which tab is the sync tab while it syncs (cleared when the browser closes).
- **waterlooworks.uwaterloo.ca**: to read your applications list in that tab.
- **Co-op Tracker's address**: to hand the list to the Co-op Tracker page.
