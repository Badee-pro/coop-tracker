# Co-op Tracker Sync: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 21, 2026_

Co-op Tracker Sync is a browser extension that copies your own WaterlooWorks co-op applications into Co-op Tracker, an application tracker you run.

## What the extension reads

When you click **Sync from WaterlooWorks** in Co-op Tracker, the extension reads your applications list on `waterlooworks.uwaterloo.ca` in the tab Co-op Tracker opened. For each application this includes the job title, job ID, term, organization, application status, job status, division, location, city, number of openings, application deadline and submission date.

It only reads WaterlooWorks in a tab that Co-op Tracker opened for a sync. It does not read other tabs or other websites, and it does nothing on WaterlooWorks pages you open yourself.

## What it does with the data

The applications are passed directly to the Co-op Tracker page open in your browser, which saves them to your Co-op Tracker. The extension:

- does **not** send data to the extension author or any third party
- does **not** read or store your WaterlooWorks password or cookies
- does **not** use analytics, advertising or tracking
- does **not** sell or share data

The extension only keeps a temporary list of which tab is a sync tab, in browser session storage. This is cleared when the browser closes.

## Permissions

- **tabs**: to recognize the WaterlooWorks tab that Co-op Tracker opened, and close it when the sync is done.
- **storage**: to remember which tab is the sync tab while it syncs.
- **waterlooworks.uwaterloo.ca**: to read your applications list in that tab.
- **Co-op Tracker's address**: to hand the list to the Co-op Tracker page.
